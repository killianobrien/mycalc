Theorem 4.2.3. Continuity of sums, products and quotients.

Suppose that functions \(f\) and \(g\) are both continuous at a point \(x=a\text{,}\) then for any \(\alpha, \beta \in \mathbb{R}\) the linear combination function \(\alpha f + \beta g\) is also continuous at \(x=a\text{.}\) Also the product function \(fg\) will be continuous at \(x=a\) as well as the quotient function \(\frac{f}{g}\) provided that \(g\) is non-zero in some surrounding interval of \(x=a\text{.}\)
