Theorem 4.2.4. Continuity of composition.

Suppose that \(f\) and \(g\) are two functions whose domains and co-domains are such that the composition function \(g \circ f\) can be defined. If \(f\) is continuous at a point \(x=a\) and \(g\) is continuous at the point \(x=f(a)\) then the composition function \(g \circ f\) is continuous at \(x=a\text{.}\)
